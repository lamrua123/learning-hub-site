    114. 15 nguyên tắc Launch V1

    01 Launch does not require perfection.

02 Core journeys matter more than feature count.

03 Test journeys, not only components.

04 Published educational content must be reviewed.

05 Test with real children and parents.

06 Accessibility is part of launch readiness.

07 Test real devices and weak conditions.

08 Security, privacy and child safety are launch gates.

09 Never trust frontend authorization.

10 Payment must be tested end to end.

11 Analytics must be tested like software.

12 Monitoring and support exist before public exposure.

13 Prepare rollback before deployment.

14 Increase exposure as evidence improves.

15 Launch is the beginning of operations, not the end of development.
